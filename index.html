What is ShivYog

अहं ब्रह्मास्मि · Aham Brahmasmi

A Path From the
Finite to the Infinite

ShivYog is an ancient spiritual science — a journey inward towards the source of infinite potential that lies dormant within every human being. It is not a religion. It is a direct experience of consciousness.

ध्

Dhyan — Deep Meditation

Ancient techniques to quieten the mind and access states of profound stillness, healing, and awareness that lie beyond ordinary thought.

ध्यानं निर्विषयं मनः
Meditation is the mind free of all objects — Vivekachudamani
प्र

Prana — Life Force Healing

Learn to channel and direct prana — life force energy — to heal the physical body, clear karmic blocks, and elevate your state of being.

प्राणो ब्रह्म
Prana is Brahman — Chandogya Upanishad
सि

Siddha — Mastery of Self

The ultimate goal of ShivYog — self-realisation. The awakening to your true nature as infinite, boundless consciousness.

अयमात्मा ब्रह्म
This Self is Brahman — Mandukya Upanishad
✦ ॐ ✦
The Five Sheaths of Existence

Pancha Kosha

पञ्चकोश · The Five Bodies

In ShivYog, the human being exists simultaneously across five bodies — from the physical to the bliss body. Healing all five is the path to complete transformation.

The Nine Vedic Planets · The Five Koshas · The Sri Yantra

Yatha Pinde Tatha Brahmande
As is the microcosm, so is the macrocosm — Yajur Veda

Core Teachings

तत् त्वम् असि · Tat Tvam Asi

The ShivYog
Path

Six pillars of transformation that take the seeker from mindfulness to self-realisation, step by gentle step.

01
ॐ

Mindfulness & Presence

Most humans live on autopilot. ShivYog begins by gently awakening awareness — teaching us to be fully present in each moment, free from the tyranny of unconscious thought.

उत्तिष्ठत जाग्रत प्राप्य वरान्निबोधत
Arise, awake and learn by approaching the wise — Katha Upanishad
02
श्री

Shri Vidya Sadhana

The sacred practice of Advait Shri Vidya — a profound meditative discipline transmitted in an unbroken lineage from the Himalayan masters to sincere seekers.

श्रद्धावान् लभते ज्ञानम्
One who has faith obtains divine knowledge — Bhagavad Gita 4.39
03
प्र

Pranic Healing

Ancient healing modalities that work directly with the subtle body — clearing energy blockages, releasing stored trauma, and restoring the natural flow of life force.

सर्वं खल्विदं ब्रह्म
All this is indeed Brahman — Chandogya Upanishad 3.14
04
क

Karma Dissolution

Understanding and consciously dissolving karmic patterns that perpetuate suffering — transforming unconscious cycles into conscious evolution and genuine freedom.

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन
You have a right to perform your actions, not to the fruits — Gita 2.47
05
भक्

Bhakti — Devotion

The path of the heart. Bhakti dissolves the ego through love — opening the seeker to a direct experience of grace that transcends intellectual understanding.

भक्त्या मामभिजानाति
Through devotion one truly knows Me — Bhagavad Gita 18.55
06
मु

Mukti — Liberation

The culmination of the ShivYog path — abiding in the state of pure awareness, beyond birth and death, suffering and seeking. This is your true nature.

मोक्षस्य न हि वासः
Liberation has no fixed abode — it is your own true nature — Ashtavakra Gita
The journey from finite to infinite is one that every human being must make.
— Dr. Avdhoot Shivanand Babaji
